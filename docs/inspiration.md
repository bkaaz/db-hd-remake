# Inspiration

**We borrow, we do not copy.** The fight is inspired by the 3D games of the
Budokai line: Budokai 3, Burst Limit and Infinite World. It will come out as
something in between: a mix of their mechanics, the owner's own ideas, and
whatever plays well on our 2D sprites. Nothing here is a rule.
[`combat.md`](./combat.md) says what the rules are, and a mechanic only becomes
one when the owner settles it there. This file is a reference shelf: what those
games do, in our words, so a conversation can point at it.

Anything not confirmed by a source is marked `?`.

## Burst Limit (Xbox 360, 2008)

**The pad.** This is the default ("A Type") layout from the manual's controller
page.

| button | what it does |
|---|---|
| D-pad / left stick | move; double-tap and hold to dash |
| A | guard; tapped as a melee blow lands, a dodge |
| B | ki blast, held to charge it; with ← or → a super attack, held longer for more power; with ↑ and a full ki gauge, the ultimate |
| X | rush attack (the string) |
| Y | smash attack (the heavy one) |
| A + X | grab |
| A + Y | taunt |
| LB | aura spark (with a full ki gauge); otherwise grab |
| RB | transform (with a full ki gauge); otherwise grab |
| LT | blow-away attack |
| RT | pursuit; also ultimate guard |
| START | pause |

The manual's options screen offers two preset layouts (A Type, B Type) and a
custom one, where each button is given a move from a list.

**Mechanics the manual names:**
- **Defence.** Holding guard blocks. Tapping guard as a blow lands dodges it. A
  basic ki blast is deflected back by the same timing. Away plus guard, just as
  a blow hits, is the *ready stance*: a teleport behind the attacker. After a
  knockdown, any face button rolls.
- **Fatigue gauge.** It fills when taking damage, blocking, dodging, or using
  the ready stance. When it is full the fighter can briefly not move.
- **Ki gauge.** It fills by itself over time. Specials spend it.
- **Aura actions** cost one bar of ki and go on RT: RT + Y is a heavy smash,
  RT + B a *mega crush* (a ball of energy that pushes the opponent back), and RT
  after a knockback is a *pursuit attack*. Pursuits chain when RT and a
  direction are pressed again. Holding RT with ← / → + B is an *EX super*, a
  charged super without the charging.
- **Aura spark** (LB with a full gauge) is a short powered mode that drains the
  gauge when it ends. In it, LB with ← / → vanishes behind the opponent, LB
  alone is a vanishing attack, and holding LT after a launch starts the
  ultimate.
- **Drama pieces**: cut-scenes in the middle of a fight. Out of scope for us.

## Budokai 3 (PS2, 2004)

Four buttons: punch (□ ?), kick (△ ?), ki (○ ?) and guard (✕). Only guard on ✕
is confirmed by the fan FAQ we read.

- **Strings** mix punch and kick freely. A direction changes some blows. The
  last blow of a string can be held to charge it, and fully charged it breaks
  guard.
- **Guard**: held, it blocks. Tapped, it dodges. Guard with up or down
  side-steps. Towards plus guard, as a blow lands, is the *teleport counter*.
- **Dash**: tap forward and hold to dash, tap forward once to leap. Tap back to
  dash back.
- **Throw**: punch + guard.
- **Ki**: a gauge of bars. A higher level adds a little attack and a lower one
  costs a little defence. Powering up is a guard-held motion (?).
- **Hyper mode**: all four buttons at once. The fighter glows, ki drains, and
  light blows stop staggering him. In hyper mode, ki after a knockback starts a
  *Dragon Rush*, a short guessing game played on the face buttons.

## Infinite World (PS2, 2008)

Built on Budokai 3's engine with things carried over from Burst Limit. The
owner liked its dash.

- **R1, the aura dash**: a burst of speed in any horizontal direction (not up
  or down). It is used to make space or to get behind the opponent. Its ki cost
  is not stated (?).
- **L1** charges ki while held, and it is not instant. Guard + L1 is an aura
  guard: it blocks everything but throws, at a large cost in ki.
- **R2** (or punch + kick) is a *blow back*: it cancels one incoming blow.
  During an aura dash it becomes a heavy slam.
- **L2**, *ki burn*: it drains ki quickly and is used to teleport-counter and to
  chase a teleporting opponent.
- Ki drains slowly through the fight by itself.
