## The original game

- **Title:** Dragon Ball Z: Hyper Dimension
- **Platform:** SNES / Super Famicom
- **Year:** 1996 (Japan); later PAL/European release
- **Developer:** TOSE
- **Publisher:** Bandai
- **Genre:** 2D one-on-one versus fighting game

## Confirmed facts (sourced)

Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Z:_Hyper_Dimension).
Verified 2026-08-07.

- It is a **2D one-on-one fighter**.
- **Roster — 10 playable fighters:** Goku, Gohan, Piccolo, Majin Vegeta, Vegito,
  Gotenks, Majin Buu, Kid Buu, Frieza, Perfect Cell.
- **Controls:** four-button scheme; special moves and "meteor combos" are
  executed via button combinations.
- **Health/Ki system:** the lifebar acts as **both health and Ki meter**.
  Executing special moves **decreases** the character's health, while
  **recharging Ki increases** it. (A single resource, not separate bars.)
- **Rush Battle System:** Ki-blast counterattacks that trigger when both fighters
  strike at the same time (the game's power-struggle / clash mechanic).
- **Desperation moves** are available at low health.
- Ability to **send the opponent to the back of a stage**.
- **Three play modes.** Story mode is **excluded in the European version**.
- Developer TOSE; publisher Bandai; 1996 (Japan), later PAL release.
- An English fan-translation patch exists (Twilight Translations, 2010) — useful
  as a reference.
